| Homology Type       | Applicability                                                                   | Computation     |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------- |
| Singular Homology   | All topological spaces                                                          | Hard to compute |
| Simplicial Homology | Only triangulable spaces (i.e. geometric realizations ofe simplicial complexes) | Easy to compute |

CW complexes are obtained by gluing spherical cells, more general than triangulable spaces, but computation of the singular homology groups is often more tractable.
