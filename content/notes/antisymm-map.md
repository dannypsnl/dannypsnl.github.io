The antisymmetrization map $"Alt"^k : T^0_" k"(V) -> L^k_"alt"(V)$ is defined by

$$
"Alt"^k (omega)(v_1,dots,v_k) :=
(1)/(k!) sum_(sigma in S_k) "sgn"(sigma) omega(v_sigma_1,dots,v_sigma_k)
$$

> [!note]
> This a is map from [[tensor|tensors]] to [[Alternating k-multilinear map|space of alternating maps]].
